Privacy Policy

My Little Studio is a coloring app made for children. We built it so families can use it with confidence: the app collects no personal information at all.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for or collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact details
  • Photos, voice recordings, or location
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers

There are no accounts to create, no sign-in, no chat, and no way for a child to talk to strangers inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your child's paintings, progress, and settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere. Paintings never appear in the phone's photo gallery unless you choose to share one yourself.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads in My Little Studio, and no third-party trackers or analytics. The app does not use any advertising or tracking software.

Children's privacy

Because the app is designed for children, we follow the rules that protect them, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's privacy rules in Europe (GDPR-K). Since we collect no personal information from anyone — child or adult — there is nothing to share, sell, or keep. Parents are always in control: the Parent area is protected by a press-and-hold gate, and optional content packs stay off unless a parent turns them on.

Links

The app contains no links to outside websites, except this policy page. It never opens a web browser on its own.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. The version shown here is always the current one.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at info@mylittlestudioapp.com.

Refund Policy

We want every family to be happy with My Little Studio. How a refund works depends on where you bought it:

Bought in an app store

If you bought My Little Studio through Google Play or the Apple App Store, your purchase and any refund are handled by that store, under its own refund policy. Please request a refund through Google Play or Apple — we cannot issue refunds for store purchases ourselves.

Bought directly from us

If you bought My Little Studio directly from Daily Word 360 (for example through our online shop), email us at info@mylittlestudioapp.com within 14 days of your purchase.

If the app is not working on your device, tell us first — we will do our best to fix it. If we cannot get it working for you, we will refund your purchase.

Before you ask for a refund

A quick check first: make sure the app is fully installed and your device has enough free storage. Most problems are fixed by updating the app or restarting the device — and we are always glad to help at info@mylittlestudioapp.com.
