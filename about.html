About My Little Studio

Color & Play

A coloring desk, right on your screen

My Little Studio turns your phone or tablet into a real painting desk. Your child sits down at the desk, picks a page, and colors it in — just like with crayons and paper, only nothing gets messy and every painting is kept safe in the app's own gallery.

It is made for children ages 3 to 8, and it works on phones, tablets, and computers.

How it works

There are 100 coloring pages in 10 stages, with 10 pages in every stage. As your child finishes pages, new art tools are unwrapped — just like opening a new box of crayons.

  • Stage 1 — Crayons
  • Stage 2 — Colored Pencils
  • Stage 3 — Markers
  • Stage 4 — Watercolors
  • Stage 5 — Pastels
  • Stage 6 — Stickers
  • Stage 7 — Gel Pens
  • Stage 8 — Glitter Gel Pens
  • Stage 9 — Stamps
  • Stage 10 — Master Studio — every tool, plus a blank canvas

In each stage, finishing 6 pages unlocks the next stage. The other 4 pages are bonus pages — fun extras that are never needed to move on. Finishing all 10 pages in a stage earns a gold Stage Complete badge.

Special gift: when your child reaches Stage 3, they receive a glitter brush they can use on any page from then on.

Every page has a color guide

Each page comes with its own color guide, so your child always knows which colors to use — and they can zoom right in to color the smallest details. Every shape is fully enclosed, so colors stay neatly inside the lines.

Safe by design

My Little Studio has no ads, no chat, no accounts, and no way for a child to talk to strangers. It collects no personal information at all, and it works offline. Paintings stay inside the app — they are never saved to the phone's photo gallery unless you choose to share one yourself.

Parents are always in control: the Parent area is protected by a press-and-hold gate, and the optional page packs stay off unless a parent turns them on.

Optional packs for parents

Three extra packs can be switched on from the Parent area, at no extra charge:

  • Bible-Story Pages — 7 Bible-story coloring pages
  • Mythical Creatures Pages — dragons, unicorns, and friends
  • Superhero Pages — original heroes only, no Marvel or DC characters

These packs are completely optional and never block progress.
